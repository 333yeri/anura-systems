**Camera angle:** Eye level (1.6m height), looking forward and slightly down at the muddy path. The path curves gently to the right.

## Quick reference: What to ask Gemini for adjustments

- **"Too bright"** → Add: "lower exposure", "darker overall", "dimmer lighting"
- **"Too foggy/hazy"** → Add to negative: "fog, haze, mist, atmospheric haze, god-rays"
- **"Trees too bright green"** → Add: "very dark muted greens, almost black-green, no bright foliage"
- **"Need more/less canopy"** → Specify: "60% canopy coverage" or "open canopy with moon visible"
- **"Wrong angle"** → Specify: "looking forward and slightly down" or "looking up at the canopy"
